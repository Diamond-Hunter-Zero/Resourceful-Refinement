This page is the design-intent index for the v0.3 crafting **materials** and the machine-recipe tables that produce and consume them. It captures the intended progression (materials, distillery/mixing/refinery/sieve/sequenced-assembly chains, and the Brewer's Tap drink system) and reconciles each entry against what currently ships in `data/resourceful_refinement/recipe/`. Most materials and their recipes are implemented; a few tables below are still design targets and are flagged accordingly. Fluid-specific chains are documented in more depth in [[Fluid Processing Recipes]].

**Material descriptions (design intent, preserved):**

**Polymer Residue** — Refined from mixing unrefined carborax and water over heat. Used to craft plastics, or refined carborax fluids.

**Organic Slush** — Distilled from different plant tags and water. Used for fertiliser, diesel, and tea.

**Compacted Biomatter** — Mixed from Organic Slush and string.

**Polymer Sludge** — Distilled from polymer residue.

**Graphite** — Mixed from Catalysed Sparkpowder and charcoal.

**Graphene Mesh** — Sequence-Assembly from molten scorchia and graphite.

> [!note] Implementation
> - **Organic Slush** and **Polymer Sludge** are **fluids** (`resourceful_refinement:organic_slush`, `resourceful_refinement:polymer_sludge`), not items — the mixing/distillery recipes consume/produce them as fluids. The Distillery table below refers to the same fluid as "organic_mush"/"organish_slush"; the code fluid is `organic_slush`.
> - **Graphite** ships as a **superheated mixing** of `minecraft:coal` (not charcoal) + 100 mB `catalysed_sparkpowder` → **2** graphite (`recipe/mixing/graphite_mixing.json`). The intent text (charcoal) and the mixing table below (50 mB catalysed_sparkpowder → 2 graphite) differ from the shipped 100 mB.
> - **Paint Blob** (`paint_blob`) is registered as an item but has **no recipe** in v0.3 data — treat its acquisition as **Planned** until a coating/paint-nozzle recipe is added.

## Implemented material recipes

These ship in `data/resourceful_refinement/recipe/` and are the authority for current behaviour:

- **Ferrous Crystal** — [[Forge Mould]] casting: `amethyst_shard` + 500 mB `molten_scorchia` → 2 (`amethyst_to_ferrous.json`); `quartz` + 500 mB `molten_scorchia` → 4 (`quartz_to_ferrous.json`).
- **Flux Dust** — mixing: `redstone` + 75 mB `silica_substrate` → 6 (+4 at 50% chance), 100 t (`flux_dust_mixing.json`).
- **Durasteel Alloy** (fluid) — mixing: 16 `ferrous_crystal` + 1000 mB `molten_crimsite` + 1000 mB `molten_asurine` → 250 mB `durasteel_alloy`, 200 t (`durasteel_alloy.json`).
- **Durasteel Ingot** — mixing: `ingot_mould` + 500 mB `durasteel_alloy` → 1, 120 t (`durasteel_ingot_mixing.json`); or [[Forge Mould]]: `ingot_mould` (25% consumed) + 250 mB `durasteel_alloy` → 1 (`durasteel_alloy_to_ingot.json`).
- **Durasteel Sheet** — [[Forge Mould]] casting: 250 mB `purified_durasteel` → 1 (`purified_durasteel_to_sheet.json`).
- **Graphite** — mixing: `coal` + 100 mB `catalysed_sparkpowder` → 2, superheated, 360 t (`graphite_mixing.json`).
- **Graphene Mesh** — sequenced assembly on `graphite`: 3 loops of (fill 25 mB `molten_scorchia` → press) via `incomplete_graphene_mesh`, 11% `graphene_mesh` / 3% `coal` (`graphene_mesh_assembly.json`).
- **Compacted Biomatter** — mixing: `string` + 500 mB `organic_slush` → 2, 80 t (`compacted_biomatter_mixing.json`).
- **Polymer Residue** — mixing: 500 mB `unrefined_carborax` + 500 mB `water` → 4, heated, 300 t (`polymer_residue_mixing.json`).
- **Drinks Glass** — shaped crafting: 3× `c:glass_panes` (`drinks_glass_shaped_crafting.json`).
- **Advanced Pump** — shaped crafting: `create:mechanical_pump` + `redstone` + `polymer_residue` + 2× `dried_kelp` (`advanced_pump_shaped_crafting.json`). See [[Advanced Pump]].

## Brewer's Tap & Drinks

**Brewer's Tap** — Extracts fluids directly from adjacent distilleries/tanks. Can be right-clicked with an empty hand to toggle between open or closed. Supports a single item-slot. Right-clicking with any item places it in that slot, or swaps the current item. This item is an optional 'flavour' ingredient, which can be included in recipes.

When an open tap is placed above a depot with a valid item, it drains fluid and creates the specified item on the depot. The flavour item is consumed if part of the recipe.

**Mixed Drink (Data Component)** — Lists a 'drink base' and 'flavour'. Drink-base determines food stats. Flavour determines effects.

> [!note] Implementation
> Implemented — see [[Brewer's Tap]]. The `brewers_tap` recipe type ships milkshake, hot chocolate, energy drink, mead, spirits and tea tappings, and the drink items (`milkshake`, `hot_chocolate_mug`, `energy_drink`, `mead_tankard`, `spirits_bottle`, plus `glue_pot`) carry a `flavour` data component that sets colour, name and effects.
